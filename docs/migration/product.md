
# Product — Fréscopa (brand intent behind the approved D1 "The Ritual" design)

## Register

**brand**

## Users

_provenance: inherited from current/PRODUCT.md._ Home coffee drinkers, MyBarista subscribers and would-be subscribers, gift buyers, and walk-in customers using the store locator.

## Product Purpose

Sell coffee, tea, machines and accessories and turn browsers into MyBarista subscribers. The homepage should make someone *want a cup*, then route them to the quiz, the subscription or the shop.

## Brand Personality

_provenance: rebrand resolution; basis: user direction "something more visually interesting", chosen concept "Ritual in motion"._

Theatrical, warm, sensory. Coffee as a ritual with a beginning, middle and end: dawn, aroma, pour, cup. Confident scale and contrast, with real product and people photography as the heroes. Playful where it earns it, never cute.

## Anti-references

- Small, tidy refreshes of the existing layout (the brief was "more visually interesting")
- Cold SaaS storefronts, stock-photo-plus-double-CTA heroes
- Clip-art promotions (stamps, starbursts)
- Generic card grids as page structure
- Third-party embeds failing in the first scroll
- The Generic-2026-SaaS silhouette

## Design Principles

1. **One idea per screen.** Each scene says one thing at full volume.
2. **Objects as heroes.** Real product photography is large, tinted into the ground, and allowed to overlap.
3. **Colour is the narrative.** The page moves from midnight roast to dawn amber, crema, coral and teal.
4. **Reuse before you write.** Every sentence is captured copy; corrections are grammar only.
5. **Motion has a still frame.** Every scroll move ends in a complete static layout; reduced-motion and no-JS get the finished page.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Body text ≥ 4.5:1, large text ≥ 3:1. One h1. `prefers-reduced-motion` and no-JS show the finished layout. Touch targets ≥ 44px. No information carried by colour alone.
